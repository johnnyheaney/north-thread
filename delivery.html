Returns & exchanges

  • Free returns within 30 days of delivery for UK orders.
  • Items must be unworn and unwashed, with the tags on.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 working days of us receiving the parcel.
  • Want a different size? Return the item and place a new order, so you're not waiting on stock.

Under UK consumer law you can cancel an online order within 14 days of receiving it; our 30-day window goes beyond that. Faulty items are always covered.

A real shop's terms should be checked by the client or their adviser.
